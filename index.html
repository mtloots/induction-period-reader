Eight runs from the collaborating laboratory's own archive are built into this page, chosen to span the awkward cases: shapes at the wall of the admissible set, shapes deep in the negative half-line, runs whose second-derivative reading does not exist, and the shortest and longest induction periods in the set. The tool refits each from its raw trace and compares against the value in the workbook that accompanies this link. If these agree, a disagreement on your own traces is a fact about the data and not about the arithmetic.

Readings are computed from the fitted curve, never by differentiating the recorded data, so noise enters through the fit where it can be accounted for. Intervals are percentile intervals from a residual bootstrap. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored: the fit runs in this browser. The engine is a JavaScript port of the arcstat C back end, agreeing with it to seven decimal places on the twenty-eight-oil archive.
